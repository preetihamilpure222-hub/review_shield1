Model 1

Fake-review detector

TF-IDF (1–2 grams, 20k features) + Multinomial Naive Bayes, trained on Amazon product reviews and hotel reviews. Outputs the probability a review is deceptive.

Model 2

Sentiment & rating predictor

TF-IDF + Logistic Regression (sentiment: {{ sent.sentiment_accuracy }}% accuracy) and Ridge regression (star rating, ±{{ sent.rating_mae }} stars). Trained on {{ '{:,}'.format(sent.train_size) }} Amazon reviews.

Layer 3

Behavioural red flags

Duplicate text, shouting, excessive exclamation, promotional phrases, very short text, and mismatch between the star rating and the wording.

Final risk score

Risk = {{ (weights[0]*100)|int }}% × AI fake probability + {{ (weights[1]*100)|int }}% × rule score. A review is Suspicious at risk ≥ {{ (th[0]*100)|int }}, Uncertain from {{ (th[1]*100)|int }}, otherwise Genuine. Thresholds are set in apps/predictor.py.

Trust score = 100 − average risk across all reviews. Trusted rating = average star rating after excluding suspicious reviews.

Forecasting

When reviews include dates, ratings (or predicted ratings) and suspicious-review share are averaged per month and a linear trend is projected 3 months ahead with a shaded range. It needs at least 4 distinct months of data and is a simple trend line, not a guarantee.

Fake-review model (v3) — held-out test performance

Two-stage stacked model: TF-IDF + Logistic Regression → Gradient Boosting on stylometric features (+ rating & verified purchase when available). Trained on 3 datasets (Amazon 21,000 · Hotel deceptive-opinion 1,600 · Social/Yelp-style 21,476).

Limitations

  • The detector was trained on Amazon, hotel and social-review data; text alone gives ~68% accuracy, so supply ratings/verified status where possible. Accuracy drops on unseen domains (see Overview), so Flipkart results are indicative.
  • Models read English text. Hinglish or regional-language reviews will be less reliable.
  • Text-only models cannot see reviewer history, purchase verification or posting bursts unless those columns are provided.
  • Always confirm important decisions manually.
